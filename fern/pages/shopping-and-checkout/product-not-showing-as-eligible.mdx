If a product isn’t showing as eligible at checkout, there are a few possible reasons. Here’s how to identify the issue and what to do next.

## **Why is my product not showing as eligible?**

HSA/FSA eligibility at checkout isn’t determined at the brand level. Even within a Truemed partner brand, eligibility is set at the product level. Some products from the same brand will show the Truemed HSA/FSA checkout option, while others won’t.

There are several reasons a product might not show as eligible. Here’s how to identify what’s happening and what you can do next.

### 1. The product is genuinely not eligible

Not every product a brand sells qualifies for HSA/FSA use. Eligibility is determined product by product based on IRS guidelines, not brand by brand. Some products may be accessories, lifestyle items, clothing, or otherwise fall outside what the IRS considers a qualified medical expense.

**How to tell if this applies:**

- There’s no HSA/FSA option at checkout, and the product is an accessory, lifestyle item, or otherwise not clearly health-related.
- The product falls outside the categories covered by IRS Section 213(d) qualified medical expenses.

**What to do:**

If the product isn’t eligible, you have a few options:

- **Find an eligible alternative.** The brand may carry a similar product that qualifies. Look for products in the same category that show the HSA/FSA checkout option.
- **Purchase with a regular card.** You can still buy an ineligible product with a standard debit or credit card. Since Truemed wasn’t involved in the transaction, the purchase won’t appear in the Orders tab of your Truemed dashboard.
- **Split your payment.** Your cart can include eligible and ineligible items, with the eligible amount paid using your HSA/FSA card and the ineligible amount paid using a personal credit or debit card. Learn more in[ *Can I Pay with Both My HSA/FSA Card and a Credit Card?*](https://help.truemed.com/shopping-checkout/can-i-pay-with-both-my-hsa-fsa-card-and-a-credit-card).
- **Browse the Truemed marketplace.** Visit[ *truemed.com/shop*](https://www.truemed.com/shop) to explore eligible products across partner brands.

### 2. The product hasn’t been categorized yet

A product may qualify for HSA/FSA use but not yet be available through Truemed because it hasn’t been reviewed and added to the system. This can happen when a partner brand launches a new product.

**How to tell if this applies:**

- The brand is a known Truemed partner, and other products from the brand show as eligible at checkout.
- The product is clearly health-related, such as a fitness tracker, a supported supplement, or a recovery device.

**What to do:**

Contact Truemed support at [support@truemed.com](mailto:support@truemed.com) and share the product name and URL. The team can work with the merchant to evaluate whether the product qualifies and, if so, get it added to the system.

### 3. You selected a different version of the product

Eligibility may vary between versions of the same product. For example, one size, color, or bundle may be eligible while another isn’t.

**How to tell if this applies:**

- You selected a different size, color, bundle, or other product variant.
- The Truemed option appeared for one version but disappeared after you changed your selection.

**What to do:**

Check the exact product version you’ve selected and see whether the Truemed option appears after you select the variant you want to purchase. If the product should be eligible but still doesn’t appear, contact support at [support@truemed.com](mailto:support@truemed.com) with the product URL and the specific version you selected.

### 4. You’re shopping on a third-party website

Truemed’s integration is built directly into partner merchants’ checkout flows. It only works when you shop on a participating brand’s own website.

If you find a Truemed-eligible product on a third-party retailer like Amazon, Best Buy, [Walmart.com](http://Walmart.com), or another marketplace, you won’t see the HSA/FSA option at that retailer’s checkout. This is true even if the exact same product is eligible when purchased directly from the brand.

**How to tell if this applies:**

- You’re shopping on Amazon, Best Buy, [Walmart.com](http://Walmart.com), or another third-party retailer.
- You’re purchasing through a third-party seller instead of the brand’s own website.

**What to do:**

Go directly to the brand’s website to make your purchase. For example, if you want an eligible Garmin device, purchase it at[ ](https://www.garmin.com)[*garmin.com*](http://garmin.com) rather than through Amazon or a third-party seller. If you’re shopping for a supplement from a brand that partners with Truemed, buy it from the brand’s direct site.

Shopping directly from the brand lets you access Truemed’s checkout and apply your HSA/FSA funds at the point of sale.

If you’ve already purchased on a third-party site, contact the retailer to see whether you can cancel the order or request a refund, then repurchase directly from the brand using Truemed at checkout.

### 5. The brand isn’t a Truemed partner

Truemed’s checkout option is only available through participating merchant brands. If you’re shopping on a brand’s direct website and don’t see a Truemed HSA/FSA option, the brand may not be integrated with Truemed.

**What to do:**

Browse the[ *Truemed marketplace*](https://www.truemed.com/shop) to find participating brands and explore eligible products.

## **Showing as Eligible vs. Qualifying for HSA/FSA**

Seeing the Truemed option at checkout means you can purchase the product through Truemed. Whether you need to complete a health survey depends on the product:

- **Pre-approved products:** You can purchase these through Truemed without completing a qualification survey.
- **Other products:** You’ll need to complete a health survey and receive a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) to qualify.

If you pay with a personal card and submit a reimbursement claim afterward, seeing the Truemed option or receiving an LMN doesn’t guarantee that your administrator will approve the claim. Your HSA/FSA administrator makes the final reimbursement decision.

Learn more in the[ *Eligibility & Qualification Overview*](https://help.truemed.com/eligibility-qualification/eligibility-qualification-overview) and[ *Why Was My Claim Denied?*](https://help.truemed.com/reimbursements-claims/why-was-my-claim-denied).

## **What if I think the eligibility result is wrong?**

If something doesn’t look right, the next step depends on what happened.

- **The product doesn’t show as eligible, but you think it should.** See reason 2 above for how to request a product eligibility review. Contact support at [support@truemed.com](mailto:support@truemed.com) with the product name and URL.
- **You completed the health survey but didn’t receive an LMN.** See[ *Why Didn’t I Get an LMN for My Purchase?*](https://help.truemed.com/reimbursements-claims/why-didnt-i-get-an-lmn-for-my-purchase) and[ *Troubleshooting Your Qualification Survey*](https://help.truemed.com/eligibility-qualification/troubleshooting-your-qualification-survey).
- **Your HSA/FSA administrator denied your reimbursement claim.** See[ *Why Was My Claim Denied?*](https://help.truemed.com/reimbursements-claims/why-was-my-claim-denied) for common reasons and next steps.

## What to do next

Here is a quick summary of next steps based on your situation:

| Situation                                                     | What to do                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product should be eligible but has no HSA/FSA option          | Contact [support@truemed.com](mailto:support@truemed.com) to request product categorization                                             |
| Shopping on Amazon, Best Buy, or another third-party site     | Go to the brand's direct website to purchase instead                                                                                    |
| Product exists on a partner brand's site but isn't eligible   | Look for an eligible alternative, or purchase with a regular card                                                                       |
| Not sure if the brand is a Truemed partner                    | Browse [truemed.com](https://truemed.com) to find confirmed partner brands and eligible products                                        |
| Logged in to Shop Pay and can’t see the Truemed option        | Log out of Shop Pay and check out as a guest                                                                                            |
| Truemed option shows, but you didn’t qualify after the survey | See[ *Why Didn’t I Get an LMN for My Purchase?*](https://help.truemed.com/reimbursements-claims/why-didnt-i-get-an-lmn-for-my-purchase) |

If you are unsure which situation applies to you, reaching out to [support@truemed.com](mailto:support@truemed.com) is always a good place to start. Include the product name, a link to the product page, and a description of what you are seeing at checkout. Truemed's support team can confirm whether the product is eligible, whether it's an uncategorized SKU, or whether you need to shop elsewhere.
