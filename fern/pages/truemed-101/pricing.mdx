## **How much does Truemed cost?**

The cost of using Truemed depends on the merchant and the service you're using. Most partner brands cover the cost of the Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN), so qualified customers can use Truemed without paying a separate LMN fee. Certain services, including some gym and fitness studio memberships, may involve a customer-paid fee.

When a fee applies, it helps cover the process of connecting your purchase to a qualifying medical need, including the clinical review and issuance of your Letter of Medical Necessity.

An LMN is a document issued by an independent, licensed medical provider confirming that a purchase is medically necessary for your specific health condition. It provides documentation to support using HSA/FSA funds for eligible expenses.

## **How much can you save with Truemed?**

Because HSA and FSA accounts use pre-tax dollars, qualified customers can save around 30% on eligible purchases, depending on their tax bracket and other individual circumstances. The savings can add up on both large purchases and recurring expenses.

#### Example 1: A $2,000 sauna

Assuming a 30% effective tax savings rate, using HSA/FSA funds on a qualifying $2,000 purchase could save approximately $600 compared with paying with after-tax income.

#### Example 2: $50 per month in supplements

If you spend $50 per month on eligible supplements, that's $600 annually. At a 30% effective tax savings rate, using HSA/FSA funds could save approximately $180 over the year.

These examples are estimates, not guaranteed savings. Your actual tax savings will vary, and purchases must qualify for HSA/FSA use.

## **How do reimbursement and direct HSA/FSA card payments work with Truemed?**

Truemed offers two ways to use HSA/FSA funds, depending on the merchant's setup.

### Direct HSA/FSA Card Payment

If Truemed is available at checkout:

1. Select Truemed as your payment method and complete the health survey.
2. Pay for your purchase with your HSA/FSA card or another accepted payment method.
3. An independent, licensed clinician reviews your information.
4. If the clinician determines you qualify, they issue your LMN, which will be available with your purchase documentation.

Your purchase is completed at checkout, and your documentation will be available in your Truemed dashboard if you're approved. While it's rare, completing the survey doesn't guarantee an LMN will be issued.

### Post-Purchase Reimbursement

If the merchant offers Truemed's reimbursement process:

1. Pay for your purchase with a regular credit or debit card.
2. Complete the health survey through the merchant's confirmation page or follow-up email, within the required timeframe.
3. If you qualify, receive your LMN.
4. Submit the LMN and receipt to your HSA/FSA administrator to request reimbursement.

Your plan administrator makes the final decision on whether to approve a reimbursement claim. Receiving an LMN doesn't guarantee approval.

  <Accordion title="Does Truemed cost anything, or is it free?">
    For most purchases, it's free. The cost depends on the merchant and service. Most Truemed partner brands cover the LMN fee, so customers don't pay a separate fee for the letter. Certain services, including some gym and fitness studio memberships, may charge a customer-paid fee. Check the terms for your specific purchase before completing the process.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Truemed's fee charged per purchase or once a year?">
    Your LMN is generally valid for 12 months and covers eligible purchases within the product category and merchant it was issued for, including subscriptions and refills. If you purchase a different product under a different category from the same merchant, you'll need a new LMN. If a gym or studio charges an LMN fee, you generally won't pay it again for covered purchases until your LMN expires.

  Whether a fee applies, and when it is charged, depends on the specific service and merchant.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a new LMN (and a new fee) for every brand?">
    You'll need a new LMN for each brand, and for each product. LMNs are tied to the specific products they were issued for. On the Truemed marketplace, a new LMN doesn't mean a new fee, since the brand covers it.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do some brands charge for the LMN and others don't?">
    Brands on the Truemed marketplace all cover the fee. Some gyms and studios use consumer-paid LMNs instead, so you pay the fee yourself. The LMN and the clinical review behind it are the same either way.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If my administrator denies the claim, am I out the money and the fee?">
    If your [claim is denied](https://help.truemed.com/reimbursements-claims/why-was-my-claim-denied), email [support@truemed.com](mailto:support@truemed.com). We'll help you understand why it was denied and, if you qualify, work with you to gather additional documentation to support your claim. Approval is ultimately up to your HSA/FSA administrator. Support options may vary by merchant, so check the terms for your purchase. Your order is still subject to the merchant's return policy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will my order be subject to sales tax?">
    Sales tax depends on the product, merchant, and applicable state and local tax rules. Using HSA/FSA funds doesn't automatically exempt a purchase from sales tax. Check your order total at checkout for the applicable taxes and fees.

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I split my payment across two cards?">
    Yes. Split payments are available at all payment merchants that integrate with Truemed. You can split your purchase between an HSA/FSA card and a personal debit or credit card if your HSA/FSA card doesn't cover the full amount. You can't split a payment across two HSA/FSA cards.
