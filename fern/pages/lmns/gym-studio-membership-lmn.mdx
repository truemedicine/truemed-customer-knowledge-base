Gym and fitness studio memberships may be eligible for HSA/FSA reimbursement with a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) issued by a licensed clinical provider through Truemed.

Memberships aren’t automatically eligible. A licensed clinician must determine that regular exercise is medically necessary to treat, manage, or prevent a diagnosed condition. Memberships for general fitness or weight goals without an underlying medical condition don’t qualify. See the[ *Eligibility & Qualification Overview*](https://help.truemed.com/eligibility-qualification/eligibility-qualification-overview) for general eligibility rules.

## **What types of memberships may qualify?**

Depending on your medical needs and clinical assessment, the following types of memberships may qualify:

- Traditional gym memberships
- Boutique fitness studios offering cycling, HIIT, or strength training
- Yoga and Pilates studios
- Class-based subscriptions

See the partner list below for specific gyms and studios that work with Truemed.

## **Conditions that may support qualification**

Exercise may be part of a treatment plan for conditions such as:

- Cardiovascular conditions
- Metabolic conditions
- Musculoskeletal conditions or chronic pain

An independent, licensed clinician assesses each person's circumstances individually to determine whether a membership is medically necessary. Learn more at [*Can You Use an HSA for a Gym Membership?*](https://www.truemed.com/blog/hsa-eligible-gym-memberships)

## **What your LMN needs to establish**

To support HSA/FSA reimbursement for a gym or fitness studio membership, your LMN must establish:

- **A diagnosed medical condition** that supports the need for treatment.
- **A recommendation for regular exercise** as part of your treatment plan.
- **The medical necessity of the membership** for treating or managing your condition, rather than exercise being generally beneficial.

## **What happens after you get your LMN?**

Once your LMN is approved and issued by a clinical provider:

1. **Download your LMN** from your Truemed dashboard.
2. **Gather your purchase receipt** from your gym or studio. The receipt should be itemized and clearly show the membership fee. 
3. **Log in to your HSA/FSA administrator’s portal.**
4. **Submit both your LMN and receipt** as documentation for your reimbursement claim.

For step-by-step instructions specific to your HSA/FSA administrator, see[ *Submitting Your Claim by Administrator*](https://help.truemed.com/reimbursements-claims/submitting-your-claim-by-administrator).

For recurring memberships, your LMN is typically valid for 12 months. Submit a receipt for each billing period and renew your LMN when it expires. Learn more about recurring payments in[ *Can I Use My HSA/FSA Card on Subscriptions?*](https://help.truemed.com/shopping-checkout/can-i-use-my-hsa-fsa-card-on-subscriptions)
