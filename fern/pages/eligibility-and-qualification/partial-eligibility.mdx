Some products qualify for only part of their purchase price to be paid for or reimbursed with HSA/FSA funds, while other orders contain a mix of eligible and ineligible items. This guide explains how Truemed determines the eligible amount, what to expect at checkout, and how to submit a claim for the correct amount.

## What is partial eligibility?

Partial eligibility applies in two situations:

- **A single product has both everyday and medical uses.** Only the amount above the cost of a comparable basic version may qualify for HSA/FSA reimbursement.
- **An order contains eligible and ineligible items.** Only the eligible items can be paid for or reimbursed with HSA/FSA funds.

Under IRS Section 213(d), HSA/FSA funds can only be used for qualified medical expenses. Claiming more than the eligible amount could result in a denied claim or require you to repay funds.

## How is the eligible amount determined?

Truemed's clinical operations team determines the eligible portion of a purchase by comparing the product to a standard, comparable version of the same item. This is called the **comparable item** approach.

Here's how it works:

- A standard version of the product (without premium upgrades) is identified as the reference point
- The price of that standard version represents the ineligible base cost
- The remaining amount above that base, which reflects the medically relevant features, becomes the eligible amount

You don’t need to calculate the eligible amount yourself. Truemed’s provider team determines it during the qualification process and documents the breakdown in your Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) and, when applicable, your itemized receipt.

For mixed orders, the eligible items are separated from the ineligible items so you know which costs can be covered by HSA/FSA funds.

## Common examples

Partial eligibility comes up most often in dual-purpose product categories, where a basic version meets an everyday need and the specific product purchased adds medically relevant features on top of that baseline.

- **Mattresses:** Only the amount above the cost of a comparable basic mattress may qualify for a customer with a qualifying medical need.
- **Treadmills:** Only the amount above the cost of a comparable basic treadmill may qualify, depending on the product and medical need.
- **Air purifiers:** A qualifying air purifier may be partially eligible, depending on its features and the medical need.
- **Orthopedic footwear:** Only the amount above the cost of a comparable basic pair of shoes may qualify for a customer with a qualifying medical need.
- **Mixed cart:** An eligible air purifier purchased alongside an ineligible decorative cover may have only the air purifier amount qualify for HSA/FSA use.

Eligibility depends on the specific product and your qualification.

## What to expect at checkout

When you go through the Truemed checkout or qualification flow for a partially eligible product, you will see the eligible and ineligible amounts broken out clearly before you pay.

Here is what the experience looks like:

1. **Complete the qualification survey.** Before entering your payment details, you’ll complete a health survey. This helps Truemed’s provider team confirm eligibility and calculate the eligible portion.
2. **Review your eligible breakdown.** At the payment screen, your cart will display the HSA/FSA-eligible amount and the ineligible amount separately, so you know which portion can be reimbursed.
3. **Pay for your purchase.** For a partially eligible product, you pay the full purchase price with a credit or debit card, then submit the eligible portion for reimbursement. For mixed carts, eligible and ineligible items are shown separately, and you can split payment between your HSA/FSA card and a personal card. Learn more in[ *Can I Pay with Both My HSA/FSA Card and a Credit Card?*](https://help.truemed.com/shopping-checkout/can-i-pay-with-both-my-hsa-fsa-card-and-a-credit-card).

**For example:&#x20;**&#x49;f you purchase a king mattress for $2,500 and a comparable basic king mattress costs $250, you’ll pay the full $2,500 at checkout and may seek reimbursement for the eligible $2,250.

## **What documentation do I need?**

After your purchase, you can submit the eligible portion to your HSA/FSA administrator for reimbursement. Truemed provides documentation to support your claim.

Depending on your purchase, your documentation may include:

- **Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) with addendum:** Explains why the purchase is medically appropriate and documents the comparable item analysis and eligible versus ineligible amounts.
- **Truemed itemized receipt:** Shows the purchase details and, when available, the eligible and ineligible amounts.
- **Cost comparison document:** May be requested by your administrator to support the eligible amount, particularly for categories such as mattresses or footwear.

Submitting all three documents together, when available and applicable, can help reduce follow-up requests from your administrator.

If your administrator asks for documentation you don’t have, contact Truemed support at [support@truemed.com](mailto:support@truemed.com) for help.

## **How to submit a claim for a partially eligible or mixed purchase**

Once you have your LMN and receipt, follow these steps to request reimbursement from your HSA/FSA administrator:

1. **Gather your documentation.** Find your LMN, including its addendum, and your itemized receipt in your Truemed dashboard. Include a cost comparison document if one was provided or requested.
2. **Identify your HSA/FSA administrator.** This is the company that manages your account and processes reimbursement claims.
3. **Log in to your administrator’s portal.** Find the section for submitting claims or requesting reimbursement.
4. **Enter your purchase details.** Follow the administrator’s instructions and provide the required information about your purchase.
5. **Upload your documentation.** Attach your LMN, itemized receipt, and cost comparison document if applicable.
6. **Submit your claim.** Save the confirmation number or reference ID so you can follow up if needed.

**Tip:** If your administrator’s claim form has a single field for the purchase amount, enter only the eligible amount shown in your Truemed documentation, not the full purchase price.

If you have questions about partial eligibility or want to understand how it applies to a specific product, contact the Truemed support team at [support@truemed.com](mailto:support@truemed.com).
